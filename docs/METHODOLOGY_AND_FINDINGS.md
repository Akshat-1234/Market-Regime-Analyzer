# Methodology, empirical findings, and limitations

Status: **exploratory; not a release or prospective validation**. This is a price-index research exercise, not trading advice. Prior research is preserved in `historical/arena-market-regime-platform.zip` and `historical/original-project-31902b7.zip`; see `historical/README.md`. New results are in separate experiment directories.

## Data and information set

`research/data/nifty_index.csv` is the user-supplied file, SHA-256 `ea6dfffb93e5eabd8b6a2f96cd40d6b3ec5c7f47f2f9e6c0cbd3ca9a5f2ac5e4`, 4,684 dates 2007-09-17–2026-10-22. Its source vendor and licence were not independently established. It is an index, not a total-return investable instrument; dividends, taxes, impact and financing constraints are omitted. Data-quality output is saved per year. Very small overnight gaps in some periods and close-to-intraday correlations should be checked with the vendor before using Open or range information. Weekday absences can be exchange holidays. The original backend CSV ends earlier and is not used here.

Trailing features only. Primary HMM uses daily simple return, trailing 20-day annualized realized volatility and trailing 252-day drawdown. Warm-up rows are excluded; see saved `config.yaml`, `data_quality.csv`, `fits.csv` and copied `features.py`. Each year's scaler and model are trained strictly on preceding feature-valid data. Forecasting regime probabilities uses the forward filter, initialized with the final training posterior; it does not use smoothed future information. A decision at close t executes at the next session close and earns the following close-to-close return. Cash earns 6.5% annualized, turnover costs 10 bps one-way, fractional long/cash allocations are simulated self-financing. Same-close research in the archive is separately retained and is optimistic.

## Reproduced baseline (experiment `baseline_20261104`)

The directory alias `baseline_20261104` preserves the generated run status ID `mra-baseline-reproduction` from the original temporary output path. Re-executed archived engine using 2012-01-01 start, one-session extra execution delay, 1,000 paired 20-session block-bootstrap draws, seed 42 for bootstrap and HMM seeds 2, 42. Four specs: `rv_2`, `rv_3`, `rvd_2`, `rvd_3`, all diagonal covariance, expanding annual refits; `rvd_3` is primary for characterisation. HMM ensemble exposure is the equal mean of the four models, and HMM + Trend is the 50/50 arithmetic blend of that exposure and Trend 50, not a gate. The full configurations, source snapshot, data snapshot, fit diagnostics, probabilities, signals and daily backtests are in the run folder. The first OOS signal is 2012-01-03, first earned return 2012-01-05; final return 2026-10-22 (3,632 observations). Results below are saved outputs, rounded:

## Characterisation (experiment `characterization_20261104`)

The run's forward-filtered `rvd_3` probabilities are aligned to the same-day close. Ranks are ordered at each refit by training-posterior-weighted next-day volatility; equal ranks across years do not imply equal latent identities. We report pooled local ranks, not a single stationary state. Mean and quantile characteristics use posterior weights; episode duration, transitions, confidence and ambiguity use argmax hard assignments. Episodes and empirical transition counts reset at annual refits, making episode lengths censored. Normalized entropy is `-sum(p log p)/log(K)`; ambiguity means maximum posterior < 0.6. Rare flags mean fewer than 100 assigned observations or under 5% of OOS signals; these are heuristics, not inferential thresholds.

Rank 0/1/2 had 2,035 / 1,431 / 168 hard assigned signal observations; posterior-weighted annualized 20-day volatility was approximately 11.67% / 15.46% / 35.52%. Rank 2 is rare, and these summaries do not establish a stable economic state. The dashboard describes return, tails, trend, drawdown, downside volatility, confidence, empirical durations and transitions, and OHLC-derived range separately. Range and gap are post-fit diagnostics, not HMM input. Full-sample volatility percentile is descriptive and must not be used in a live decision. The analysis file gives all measures and input/signal/source hashes. No uncertainty interval or seed identity-stability interval is reported for these pooled state descriptions.

Forward 1/5/20/60-session close returns and positive frequencies are reported separately. They overlap and are descriptive, with no inferential intervals or multiple-testing correction. Missing horizons at the dataset end are excluded, not filled. A high conditional return does not label a contemporaneous state as recovery and does not establish a causal or predictive effect. Forward volatility, drawdown, transition-conditioned inference, stationary bootstrap, and corrected significance are not yet implemented.

## Exploratory model and feature investigation (`model_grid_20261104`)

Predefined within this implementation, but **not preregistered before the broader historical research**: joint grid of RV = daily return + volatility 20 and RVD = RV + 252-day drawdown; 2/3/4 states; diagonal/full covariance; seeds 2 and 42; 15 expanding annual folds, 2012–2026. Same common feature-valid start is used. A seed is rejected on failed convergence, invalid covariance/likelihood or <2% hard training occupancy; the highest accepted training likelihood is selected per fold and specification, never test performance. All seed attempts and reasons are saved. Held-out log density is conditional on the last training filter, in original feature units with the scaling Jacobian; compare *only within the same feature vector*. Feature ablation cannot be judged from raw log densities across different dimensionalities. No unseen holdout exists.

All 180 specification-folds had at least one accepted seed; one of 360 seed fits was rejected for low occupancy. In this retrospective grid 4-state full had the highest sample-weighted OOS mean log density in each feature family (RV 5.584; RVD 7.507; values cannot be compared between families). This is not a globally optimal model: it produced an unoccupied test state in 12 of 15 folds in each family, and RVD full 4-state mean Hungarian-aligned training-label agreement across seeds was only 0.764. For comparison, RVD 2-state diagonal had 4 zero-occupancy folds and 0.996 mean training-label agreement. Agreement is in-sample, not a validation of economic identity, and fold results are non-independent expanding samples. Higher density does not establish interpretability or predictive/strategy value. `fits.csv`, `fold_scores.csv` and `config.json` capture each fold, rejection, seed, period and specification. Fold-level seed sensitivity is not full temporal matching or uncertainty quantification. That older grid ran no HSMM or Student-t specification; geometric-duration adequacy remains unresolved. Task 4 separately evaluated Student-t diagonal candidates under its own frozen protocol, described below.

## Task 4 nested regime discovery (`task4_v1_0_0_recovery1`)

Task 4 is separate from both the three-state baseline and the exploratory model grid above. Its frozen v1.0.0 protocol used close-only daily return and 20-day volatility, K=2–5 Gaussian diagonal/full and Student-t diagonal candidates, three seeds, three chronological inner validation blocks, and 14 expanding annual outer tests (2012–2025). Training-only scaling, raw-space predictive-density conversion with the Jacobian, causal terminal-filtered outer continuation, and training-only rectangular state alignment were enforced. The recovery retained all 1,512 inner attempts and 14 selected outer refits (1,526 total), all accepted and converged. The immutable run and derived analysis are verified by their own manifests; see [Task 4 results](TASK4_RESULTS.md), [protocol](TASK4_PROTOCOL.md), and [analysis](TASK4_ANALYSIS.md).

K=5 was selected in every outer fold: Gaussian full six times, Gaussian diagonal four times, and Student-t diagonal four times. However, the exact model form changed in 8 of 13 adjacent-year comparisons. Of 70 outer fold-state observations, 26 had zero hard assignments and 13 of 14 years had at least one empty state. Another 58 carried the existing rare heuristic, which is distinct from zero occupancy. Across subsequent fold pairs, 41 state mappings were accepted, 16 ambiguous, and eight new/unmatched; all five 2025 mappings were ambiguous and remained unforced. The initial 2012 structural initialization is excluded from these subsequent-pair totals. Independent executions produced 98/98 byte-identical core fold artifacts and 42/42 matching global fold objects. The initial aggregation failure and lost in-memory global candidate attempts remain disclosed; those records were not reconstructed.

After verifying the analysis manifest, the dashboard derives each rank-1 minus rank-2 inner raw-space log-density gap from `candidate_rankings.csv`: 11 of 14 are below 0.005 per observation. Selected-seed frequencies were seed 2 in three folds, seed 42 in seven, and seed 7 in four. These are inner-selection sensitivity diagnostics, not outer performance results, seed-agreement measures, or investment evidence.

**Although K=5 was selected in every outer fold, the resulting states were not consistently occupied or reliably aligned across years. The experiment supports a recurring five-component density preference under this protocol, but does not establish five stable, identifiable economic market regimes.** Task 4 did not test forecasting, allocation, strategy performance or prospective investment usefulness. The strategy metrics elsewhere in this report and dashboard belong only to the historical baseline.

## Exploratory forecasting (`forecast_20261104`)

Annual expanding ridge fits predict the future 1-, 5-, 20- and 60-session close returns at each decision close, not implementable portfolio returns. Training uses only previously OOS, filtered regime probabilities and target outcomes whose end dates are strictly before the test fold. The first forecast year is 2014 to provide at least two years of OOS probability history. Indicator features are daily return, trailing 20-day volatility, 20- and 60-day momentum and 252-day drawdown. Three fixed models use indicators alone, indicators plus two of the three local-rank probabilities, or those inputs plus normalized posterior entropy. Scaling is fit within each training fold; ridge penalty 10 was fixed in this exploratory code, not optimized out of sample. All models and horizons use the same 3,085 test dates from 2014 to 2026 with complete 60-session outcomes. See `config.json`, `predictions.csv` and `metrics.csv` for dates, label maturity, seeds and fold-level results.

OOS R² versus a fold-specific expanding training-mean forecast for indicator / indicator + regimes / indicator + regimes + entropy respectively: 1 session **-0.009 / -0.018 / -0.018**, 5 sessions **-0.015 / -0.056 / -0.059**, 20 sessions **-0.044 / -0.192 / -0.241**, 60 sessions **-0.049 / -0.217 / -0.227**. All are negative, and the additions worsen aggregate MSE in this particular retrospective experiment. This does not establish a general absence of predictability.

We synchronized 1,000 circular stationary-bootstrap samples of the daily paired squared-error differences, using expected block length 60 and seed 1701. A centered max-absolute-standardized statistic yields simultaneous 95% intervals across the eight horizon/addition comparisons. Positive differences favor regime additions. Seven intervals include zero; the 1-day regime-plus-entropy interval is entirely negative, conditional on this stationary-bootstrap approximation and limited family. Markets may be nonstationary, 60-day targets overlap, and correction does not cover earlier research or the choice of this experiment. These are not definitive confirmatory tests; no causal, trading, or prospective claim follows. Forward-risk targets, alternative algorithms and richer benchmark families remain untested.

## What is missing / next work

- **E7 not completed:** the exact four independent historical E7 specifications, state-ranking rule and prior preregistered fold/bootstrap procedure were not present. It would be misleading to substitute specifications chosen after reading results. Obtain that protocol; then calculate the prespecified high (rank 2–3) versus low (0–1) 20-session contrast across available folds with synchronized stationary-bootstrap resamples and simultaneous max-statistic correction. Claim positive evidence only if every relevant corrected 95% lower bound required by that protocol exceeds zero. None is claimed now.
- Expand the exploratory indicator-versus-regime forecasting study to risk outcomes, alternative models, market-period analysis, broader controls and robust correction for research search. Adaptive strategy-selection tests remain unimplemented.
- Allocation needs genuinely matched exposure, turnover and volatility controls and separate inference, including uncertainty in control generation; current fixed controls are insufficient. Per-fold strategy metrics and duration distribution testing are not completed in this new platform.
- No untouched prospective holdout. The archived preliminary freeze's hash `11094a...` differs from copied current source hash `c163aa...`; the archived evaluator correctly refuses it. Restore the exact frozen source and protocol (or prospectively freeze a new version *before* collecting new observations); do not relabel the 2023–2026 retrospective slice.
- More seeds, sensitivity to training windows, temporal state alignment, post-selection correction, independent data, dividend-aware investable instruments and real execution/cash modelling are needed. No HSMM is warranted solely for appearance.
