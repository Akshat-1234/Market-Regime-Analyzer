The Contrarian rejected selection based on outer likelihood, incomplete candidates silently winning by having fewer folds, and scaled-density comparisons across different training transforms. The Contrarian also required outer-mutation and continuation tests rather than prose assurances; all 21 discovery tests passed before execution.

Causality requires every decision at time `t` to use only data available before `t`. The fold design enforces train-end < validation/test-start, training-only scaling, and final terminal-alpha continuation. A local state ID is only a column label, so alignment must use training evidence and permit non-correspondence. Likelihood measures density fit, not regime meaning or economic value.

The Expansionist requested seed sensitivity, rectangular alignment, detailed occupancy/duration/entropy metrics, immutable per-fold Task 2 manifests, and a complete SHA inventory. Thresholds are acknowledged as descriptive conventions, frozen before results, with ambiguous/unmatched states preserved. The Outsider also required no semantic bull/bear labels and no conversion of a successful fit into a positive regime conclusion.

### 5. Executor

The Executor verified protocol/fold-design hashes, actual annual dates, 12 candidates × 3 seeds × 3 inner folds, immutable artifact creation, source-commit provenance, and reproducible fold artifacts. The pre-execution source must be committed before the run so run manifests can identify exact code without self-reference. ## WHERE THE COUNCIL AGREES

- The 2012–2025 expanding annual outer design and three chronological inner folds are leakage-safe by construction and executable mutation tests.
- Candidate eligibility and raw-density comparability are mathematically declared before selection.
- Outer results cannot select candidates, seeds, transformations, thresholds, or alignments.
- Local states require training-only alignment with explicit unmatched/ambiguous outcomes.
- Descriptive negative/inconclusive results are acceptable; no predictive/economic claim is authorized.

## WHERE THE COUNCIL CLASHES

The Expansionist wanted more boundary grids and looser candidate eligibility to ensure selections; the Contrarian prioritized a bounded experiment and strict evidence coverage. The frozen compromise is 14 annual folds, three inner folds, and two complete seeds, with inconclusive folds allowed. The First Principles Thinker accepted profile Hungarian alignment only because it never uses outer data and does not force ambiguous matches. No post-result threshold relaxation is permitted.

## BLIND SPOTS CAUGHT

- Scaled likelihoods from different training transforms are not automatically comparable.
- A candidate with fewer successful folds could otherwise appear superior.
- Refitting on full outer training requires a fresh training-only transform and terminal continuation state.
- Equal-K contingency alignment cannot handle changing K; rectangular unmatched handling is required.
- High self-transition is not the same as observed episode persistence.
- A pre-execution Git commit is needed for truthful run source provenance.

# CHECKPOINT C — EXECUTED RESULTS REVIEW

Checkpoint C occurred after the recovery run completed with 1,526/1,526 expected attempts, all artifact checks passed, and the recovery was compared with the preserved initial fold artifacts. It remains a structured simulated review, not independent human approval.

## Persona reviews

### 1. Contrarian

The Contrarian rejects a success narrative based on 100% convergence or repeated K=5 selection. All 1,526 fits were accepted, yet 26 of 70 outer fold-state rows had zero hard assignments and 13 of 14 years had at least one empty state. Exact candidate form changed in 8 of 13 adjacent pairs. The Contrarian also notes that 11 of 14 best-versus-runner-up inner-score gaps were below 0.005 per observation: the particular emission/covariance winner is often weakly separated. None of these results licenses predictive or economic claims.

### 2. First Principles Thinker

The causal mechanics and numerical reproducibility are established separately from the scientific interpretation. Inner-only selection, training transforms with raw-space Jacobians, terminal-filtered causal outer continuation, and training-only alignment were verified. All 98 core fold artifacts were byte-identical between independent executions. Nevertheless, occupied/identifiable components require evidence beyond fit success. K=5 is stable under the declared density criterion, but outer occupancy and cross-year identity are mixed to weak.

### 3. Expansionist

The Expansionist values the complete attempt register, annual distributions, per-state occupancy, fold-local episodes, single-seed counterfactuals, rectangular alignment outcomes, and full hashes. These expose nuance that a single selected-model table would hide. No extra feature, family, shifted boundary, or alternate outer refit is added after seeing results. The existing artifacts answer the bounded protocol; new questions require a separately versioned protocol.

### 4. Outsider

The Outsider warns against calling components “regimes” with economic names. The 2025 alignment is a useful counterexample: all five mappings were ambiguous and remained unforced. The Outsider also catches the rare-rule interpretation: `hard_count < 100 or hard_fraction < 0.05` makes rare flags common in roughly 250-row, five-state annual blocks. Thus 58/70 rare is not 58/70 empty; the independent 26/70 zero-count and dominant-state concentration results must be shown alongside it.

### 5. Executor

The Executor confirms 14 accepted outer folds, 1,512 inner attempts plus 14 selected outer refits, zero candidate failures/rejections/nonconvergence, exact aggregate reproduction, all nested Task 2 manifests, 121 recovery checksums, and nine analysis checksums. The initial aggregation failure and lost in-memory attempt list remain disclosed. The recovery does not claim access to those lost records; it verifies its own complete rectangular coverage. The initial directory remains marked failed even though its preserved fold artifacts support byte-level reproducibility comparisons.

## WHERE THE COUNCIL AGREES

- Engineering and reproducibility acceptance is met by the completed recovery, not by the preserved failed run.
- K=5 was selected in every fold and in every counterfactual single-seed winner, but that is not proof of five meaningful regimes.
- Outer empty states, concentration, candidate-form changes, ambiguous mappings, and unmatched states are negative/mixed findings and must remain prominent.
- The frozen thresholds, grid, seeds, folds, transforms, and alignment were not changed after outer outcomes.
- No forecasting, allocation, backtest, signal, semantic-label, economic-usefulness, or prospective-performance conclusion follows.

## WHERE THE COUNCIL CLASHES

The Expansionist views unanimous K=5 and 41 accepted adjacent mappings as useful structure worth future study. The Contrarian gives greater weight to 26 zero-occupancy fold-state rows, 24 non-accepted subsequent mappings, and frequent exact-model changes. The First Principles Thinker resolves the clash by separating density-grid selection from ontological regime discovery: the first is supported; the second is not established. The Outsider insists that even accepted structural IDs remain descriptive labels rather than market narratives.

## BLIND SPOTS CAUGHT

- Universal convergence can coexist with empty outer components.
- Rare is not synonymous with empty under the count-based annual diagnostic.
- High posterior confidence can accompany concentration in one component.
- Stable K does not imply stable emission, covariance, seed, state identity, or economic interpretation.
- Small winner margins make exact-form narratives fragile even when K is unanimous.
- Expected Markov duration is not observed episode duration, and annual episodes cannot be joined naively across local-label boundaries.
- Byte-identical fold outputs do not recover the initial run's lost global attempt list; that absence remains explicit.

## THE RECOMMENDATION

**ACCEPT THE EXECUTED ARTIFACTS AND REPORT A MIXED/NEGATIVE SCIENTIFIC RESULT.** State that bounded K=5 density models were consistently selected and reproduced, but the evidence does not establish five occupied, identifiable, temporally reproducible regimes. Do not retrospectively relax occupancy/alignment rules, substitute another winner, or tune boundaries. Any follow-up must be exploratory under a new frozen protocol and must not overwrite these outputs.

## THE ONE THING TO DO FIRST

Preserve and publish the complete recovery, derived diagnostics, failure/recovery disclosure, and this mixed conclusion together. Do not lead with convergence or likelihood alone; lead with the distinction between reproducible execution and unresolved regime interpretation.
